# Refund Policy

Customers can return most items within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be
unused and in its original packaging.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and are processed within 5 to 7 business days
after we receive the returned item.

Opened electronics are subject to a restocking fee of 10 percent of the purchase price. Unopened
electronics can be returned without any fee.

Digital goods such as e-books and software licences cannot be refunded once they have been
downloaded or activated.

To start a return, open the returns portal and enter your order number. Order numbers look like
ORD-48213 and are printed on your invoice and in the confirmation email.
