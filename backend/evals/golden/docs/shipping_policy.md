# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. It is free for orders above Rs 999; smaller orders
pay a flat shipping charge of Rs 49.

Express shipping delivers in 1 to 2 business days and costs Rs 149 per order, regardless of size.

We ship internationally to 12 countries. International orders take 7 to 14 business days. Customs
duties and import taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of dispatch. Orders placed after 6 pm IST are
dispatched on the next business day. We do not deliver on Sundays or national holidays.
