# Account and Security

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Two-factor authentication is available through any
authenticator app and is strongly recommended.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts an account is locked for 30 minutes. A locked account cannot be
unlocked early; the lock lifts automatically.

You can request an export of your personal data from the privacy page. Exports are prepared within
48 hours and the download link stays valid for 7 days.

Account deletion requests are completed within 30 days. After deletion, invoices are kept for 7 years
as required for tax purposes.

Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm IST, at support@example.com.
